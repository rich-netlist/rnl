# Step1 R7: candidate budget admission

Status: candidate 02 production frozen; four targeted regressions and default-path comparisons passed; final gates pending.

The independent review found that the inherited implementation could exceed the public candidate budget. Per-node bootstrap attempts, EvenRows twins and additional CURRENT frames were not consistently admitted. `Budget::candidates` and `Work::candidates` describe actual candidate attempts, rather than the final deduplicated output count. The public promise applies even though the defect also existed on main.

For a positive budget N, all candidate attempts together consume at most N slots, including one reserved P0. Budget zero permits only that necessary P0, which is reported as one unit of work. There is no per-node exception. An actual repeated estimate consumes another slot; deduplication does not refund work. Converting an already paid root Plan to an output Candidate consumes no second slot. The existing base candidate already carries the first configured CURRENT direction; only different frames are additional attempts.

Admission precedes the first order's orientation preparation. Additional barycentre orders reserve the still available slots during their batch preparation; no other consumer can change the counter in that batch. Preparation stops when all available slots have been reserved. Each resulting additional order is then formally admitted before its estimate. A duplicate barycentre order ends the finite exploration without manufacturing another candidate. This preserves the original unexhausted order and work units. Neighbour, reversed-order, EvenRows and additional CURRENT candidates use the same counter.

Candidate 01 incorrectly treated preparation of all barycentre orders as part of the first order. Independent review rejected that interpretation. Candidate 02 fixes it and measures actual orientation/barycentre calls: a flat tree with budgets 0/1/2 performs 0/0/1 orientation preparations and no barycentre preparation. The two positive slots at N=2 belong to P0 and the initial netlist order.

The targeted tests also cover nested groups with EvenRows and multiple CURRENT directions, bounded actual work at budgets 0/1/2 and further boundary values, and a real 0.3 solve/write/read cycle. The latter retains every authored fact and a zero-length connection between distinct physical pin identities, with no fallback or unreadable record.

No routing budget, pivot cap, orientation domain, retained top-two rule or objective changed. The original tiny-budget test retains its order, solver options, clock and assertions. START/END messages identify the case/level, first-solve work and fallback count, and the duration of both solves plus the checker. Its previous interrupted execution is not a pass or a timeout conclusion.
