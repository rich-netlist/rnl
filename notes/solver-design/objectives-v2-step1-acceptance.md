The necessary finding→fix information is summarized here:

- **Attachment/terminal domain:** free attachment orientations were missing from configured ranking; they now participate with their actual allowed orientations. Original anchors, explicit zero links and generated-tree/writeback guarantees remain tested; finite fallback completion is not a promise of obstacle-free output.
- **Configuration/source state:** changing or restoring a source could leave stale defaults. Settings reloads the selected configuration's exact defaults and validates the revision it will store; stale asynchronous results do not authorize another revision. Original-file provenance retains the first interpreted provider text/dependencies through version-upgrade overlays instead of reporting an empty manifest.
- **Test-oracle and budget boundaries:** Report migrations independently check each genuine `time_limit`/canonical-digest or exact provenance field difference, then preserve the rest of the complete Report, facts and identity assertions. Work-budget fixes charge candidate/LP/A* work without inventing a wall-clock bound or weakening zero-budget guards. Historical fixed-shape tests and dynamic new-default guarantees remain distinct.

Upstream #97 root selection was repaired for downstream levels declared before source cycles, with source-SCC selection, callee/cycle regression cases and the Node test wired into normal viewer CI. Test-only full-report assertions now separately validate genuine config/provenance differences; they do not broadly strip metadata. Exact historical L/exit tests preserve the old physical frame, endpoint identities and hard-phase guarantees while dynamic default facts/identity/tree tests remain.
