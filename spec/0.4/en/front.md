# RNL language standard, version 0.4

RNL (Rich Netlist) is written in the comments of a circuit netlist. It describes how the circuit is drawn as a schematic and what its parts mean, while the netlist alone defines the circuit. This document is the standard of version 0.4 of the language.

- The English text is normative. Its translations, named `rnl-0.4.<lang>.md` after a language tag such as `zh` or `ja`, are informative; where a translation differs, the English text prevails.
- The word list `rnl-words-0.4.json` and its schema `rnl-words-0.4.schema.json` are part of this standard (5.3, Annex A).
- The principles of RNL are in `rich-netlist.md` (English, authoritative; translations: `rich-netlist.<lang>.md`); this standard refers to them as "Principles".
- Versions 0.x make no promise of compatibility with each other (16.1).
- This text revises version 0.3 by the draft of version 0.4 of 2026-11-19; Annex D lists the changes.
