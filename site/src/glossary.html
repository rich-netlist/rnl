RICH NETLIST · GLOSSARY

Glossary

The English terms of RNL are the only normative ones; every language keeps its own way of rendering them.

A glossary is not part of the standard: it is a convention for translating RNL and for talking about it, kept in i18n/<lang>/glossary.json. Where a translation is unclear, the English term decides. To change a rendering, open a pull request.

RNL’s terms, such as record, usage and fill-in, are defined by the English standard; a language’s glossary only says how it writes them.
