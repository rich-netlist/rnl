标准包图库Standard packages

标准包里有什么：每个符号画出来的样子，和写出它的原文。What the standard packages hold: every symbol as it is drawn, and the text that defines it.

每份网表的根上默认就有标准包 rnl-std：它给元件配符号、给对象配样式，还给没连线的引脚补上地和电源。它由几个子包组成，一个文件可以去掉其中的子包，也可以加上按需使用的包。这一页逐个列出每个包的用途、引用关系和定义原文；符号的图是构建网站时由 RNL 的核心画的，和查看器里看到的一样。 Every netlist has the standard package rnl-std at its root by default: it gives elements their symbols and objects their styles, and fills in grounds and supplies for unwired pins. It is made of sub-packages; a file can drop any of them, and add packages that are used on demand. This page lists every package with what it is for, what it references and the text that defines it; the symbols are drawn by the RNL core when the site is built, as the viewers draw them.
