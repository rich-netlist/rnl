RNL Viewer 0.5.1 speaks eight languages, selects the net of a terminal from its label, and has clearer English texts. It is built with RNL core 0.5.2; the web viewer already has what is here, and this release brings it to the desktop viewer.

## Languages

- **View › Language** lists English and seven languages, each by its name in its own writing: Deutsch, Español, Français, 日本語, 한국어, 简体中文 and 繁體中文. A text that is not translated yet shows in English.
- German, Spanish, French, Japanese and Korean are translated by AI and may contain errors; the English terms are the normative ones. Simplified Chinese was rewritten from the English and Traditional Chinese was added, both reviewed. The native menu of the desktop viewer lists the same languages.
- **The first start follows the browser's or the system's list of languages.** The viewer takes the first language of the list that it offers, as the project site does, and else English: a browser set to Italian and then German gets German. A language you choose is remembered, and comes first.
- **A globe and the English word mark the Language menu.** When the interface is not in English, View and its Language submenu show their translated names followed by the English word in parentheses, such as "Ansicht (View)" and "Sprache (Language)", and the web page's menu shows a globe before Language, so that the way back to English can be found in a language you cannot read. The languages' own names are as they were.

## Labels of terminals

- **A label attached to a terminal now stands for the nets of its pin**, as a click on the pin does: a click on it selects the net and highlights it, and selecting a net marks the labels of the terminals on it. Before, such a label did nothing when clicked.

## Clearer English

- Texts of dialogs, menus, tooltips and errors are reworded where they were unclear: the objectives of the solver are ranked in tiers, not levels; the empty drawing and the editor send you to File › Open File, as the menu names it; the Library is the Library, not a file pool; the errors of a manifest and of an export say what to do.
- Menu labels that open a dialog end in "...", such as Include Public Package..., and Open Subcircuit in New Window, which opens at once, does not. Spelling is American (color, canceled).

Windows requires 64-bit Windows 10 or 11 and Microsoft Edge WebView2. The installers download WebView2 when it is missing. Linux requires x86_64, WebKitGTK 4.1 and a glibc version compatible with Ubuntu 22.04 or newer.

The macOS app has an ad-hoc signature only, with no Developer ID and no Apple notarization. If macOS blocks the first start of a download you trust, choose Open Anyway in System Settings › Privacy & Security, or run `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/RNL Viewer.app"`.

The downloads are not code-signed with a publisher certificate. Windows may display a SmartScreen or unknown-publisher warning. Verify downloads against `SHA256SUMS.txt`, for example with `shasum -a 256` on macOS.
